## Overview

AgriSense is an IoT agriculture platform connecting environmental sensing, live
visualization, and data-informed recommendations. I built backend software and
researched hardware for the system.

## Technical shape

The platform used Python and Flask to support the software side of an
IoT-and-sensor workflow. Its purpose was to turn environmental readings into a
live view that could support better-informed decisions rather than leaving the
data at the device layer.

## My contribution

I worked on the backend and investigated the hardware required to collect the
environmental inputs. The current public record does not establish exclusive
ownership of the complete sensing, visualization, or recommendation pipeline, so
this case study keeps the contribution boundary narrow.

## Takeaway

AgriSense reinforced that an IoT product is not complete when a sensor produces a
number. Collection, transport, presentation, and interpretation have to form one
usable path.
