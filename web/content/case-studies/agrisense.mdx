## Overview

AgriSense is an IoT agriculture platform connecting environmental sensing, live
visualization, and data-informed recommendations. I built backend software and
researched hardware for the system. The idea was in general, farmers or even regular people
don't have the capability to see whether the conditions they are growing their plants
in are optimal - AgriSense attempts to automate that process, rather than having to do
manual work.

<Callout>
  The purpose was to turn environmental readings into a live view that could support better-informed decisions rather than leaving the data at the device layer.
</Callout>

## Technical shape

The platform used Python and Flask to support the software side of an
IoT-and-sensor workflow. We used sensors such as temperature, heat,
soil quality, and moisture, and used an Arduino Uno to control responses for all
the sensors.

## My contribution

I worked on the backend and investigated the hardware required to collect the
environmental inputs. The backend architecture included pulling data from the various sensors
through the Arduino using C++, then converting that data into Python-compatible format and
setting up an API to automate this process to trigger every 10 seconds. I also figured out the best
versions of the sensors, balancing reliability, efficiency, and overall price.

## Takeaways

AgriSense reinforced that an IoT product is not complete when a sensor produces a
number. Collection, transport, presentation, and interpretation have to form one
usable path. Furthermore, being one of my first club projects at UIUC, it taught me
how to work with a team while balancing other responsibilities as a college student.
