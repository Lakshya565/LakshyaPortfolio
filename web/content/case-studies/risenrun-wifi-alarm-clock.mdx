## Overview

RiseNRun is a moving Wi-Fi alarm clock developed through COSMOS. I worked on its
embedded firmware and IoT behavior, then helped evaluate the device through
deployment and user trials.

## Technical approach

An ESP32 connected the alarm clock through Wi-Fi and MQTT while motors and
hardware controls gave it a physical response. The project paired that connected
behavior with adaptive physical tasks intended to make dismissing an alarm more
deliberate than pressing a bedside button.

## My contribution

I developed firmware and IoT behavior in C/C++ and contributed to evaluating how
the physical tasks behaved outside the initial build environment. I do not claim
sole ownership of the complete mechanical design or research program.

## Evaluation

The project progressed beyond a lab demonstration into real-world deployment and
user trials. The current public record supports that evaluation boundary but does
not provide enough verified detail for additional participant or performance
metrics.
