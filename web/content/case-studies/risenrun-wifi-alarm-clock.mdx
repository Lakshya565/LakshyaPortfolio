## Overview

RiseNRun is a moving Wi-Fi alarm clock developed through COSMOS. I worked on its
embedded firmware and IoT behavior, then helped evaluate the device through
deployment and user trials.

<Callout>
  The point was not a louder alarm. Adaptive physical tasks were meant to make dismissing one more deliberate than pressing a bedside button.
</Callout>

## Technical approach

An ESP32 connected the alarm clock through Wi-Fi and MQTT while motors and
hardware controls gave it a physical response.

## My contribution

I developed firmware and IoT behavior in C/C++ and contributed to evaluating how
the physical tasks behaved outside the initial build environment. I worked on this with 
one partner, and we equally worked to present our project at a final project symposium and 
test on real users.

## Takeaways

As this was part of a summer program, we had very limited time to work on this project and refine it.
This was my first experience developing a prototype of a larger project, as Lucky Arduino was
my only technical experience at this point. I think this project is eventually what made me strive
for bigger and better, and it was a good launchpad into more intricate experiences.
