## Overview

BackBuddy is an electronic posture brace I designed and built as a capstone. The
device used a custom circuit and PCB to detect a hunched posture and respond with
vibration, turning posture feedback into something immediate and wearable.

## Hardware approach

I developed the project around a simple physical interaction: the wearer should
not need to check a screen to understand the warning. The sensing, circuit, PCB,
and vibration response therefore had to behave as one compact hardware system.

The current public record does not preserve enough detail to claim a specific
sensor architecture or circuit topology, so this story intentionally stops at the
verified system boundary.

## Outcome

BackBuddy earned second place for engineering and product presentation among more
than thirty capstone projects. That result reflects both the working hardware and
the ability to explain why the feedback loop mattered to a potential wearer.
